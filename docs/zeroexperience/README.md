# ZeroExperience v0.1

## Promise
Sell a reliable, customizable hospitality experience rather than merely a reservation. Luxury is an experience, not a social class; courtesy and baseline service reliability apply across price tiers.

## Candlelit dinner
Guest-selected atmosphere, menu, accessibility needs, service style, music, arrival time, optional transportation and approved spending. ZeroSchedule coordinates room, kitchen, staff, lighting, energy and turnaround. ZeroDispatch verifies readiness and manages exceptions.

## Quality dimensions
Readiness, reliability, preference fit, accessibility, service pacing, privacy, guest-requested adjustments, optional satisfaction feedback and recovery after failure. Never optimize solely for speed, revenue or occupancy.

## Privacy
No covert cameras, emotional inference or individual tracking to score a romantic evening. Use operational telemetry and voluntary feedback with explicit research consent where applicable.

## Example acceptance criteria
At the confirmed arrival time the booked room and service resources are ready; exceptions are communicated promptly; guest preferences can change without exposing private reasons.
