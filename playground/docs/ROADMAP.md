# Roadmap and honest scope

Delivered first slice: navigable Metro shell, 15 customizable live tiles, named persistent layouts, dashboard palette/inspector, widget move/resize, undo/redo, validated JSON import/export, seven inspectable clankers, schedule reservations/conflicts, private role views, rituals, dinner preparation, trace inspection, synthetic faults and retries, local PBX interactions and isolated experiment comparison.

Next: transactional scenario launch, recurrence/date calendars, genuine room and vehicle reservations, private availability fixtures, occupancy/event readiness models, PBX queues, richer experiment registry, persistent research notes, full keyboard/touch audit, lazy-loaded charts and broader end-to-end tests. General purpose component gallery/Storybook and service-level target analysis are not complete.

Future physical interfaces: NFC ring identification at terminals, secure processor-backed payments, configurable finger-snap service triggers. Ordinary tag IDs are not secure payment credentials. Ambient gesture detection requires explicit opt-in and reliable identity/context. These are concepts only; no microphones, NFC, payment processors or robotics endpoints are connected.

## Compound map

The map now provides six selectable fictional areas, symbolic clanker positions derived from engine location names, live health/charging colors, fleet and path layers, fault filtering, zoom with scroll panning, an accessible area-button list, zone details and direct fleet/trace links. Unknown locations are reported as unmapped. No geolocation, personal occupancy, actual floor plan, GIS coordinates, routing or safety navigation is claimed. A real compound plan can replace the synthetic geometry later.
