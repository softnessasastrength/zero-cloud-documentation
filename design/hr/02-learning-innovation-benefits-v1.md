# ZeroCloud HR Constitution — Learning, Innovation, Experience Credits and Amenities v1

**Decision status:** Accepted project design requirements; technical/legal mechanisms pending. **Date:** 2026-11-14.

## Learning, curiosity and joy
- **Joy is a legitimate design principle of work**, not a demand for performative cheerfulness. Meaningful work, craftsmanship, autonomy, relationships, experimentation and learning are organizational goals.
- Knowledge-base and educational access are baseline employee rights regardless of rank, job title, department, productivity score or managerial favor. Limit only information legitimately protected for safety, privacy, trade secrets or legal compliance; provide an appealable explanation.
- Employees may explore training, courses, departments, corporate projects and alternative career paths during paid time.
- A separately tracked **paid exploration bank** accumulates for employee-directed curiosity. Mandatory training and compliance work cannot consume this bank.
- Employees may discover strengths outside their current role; poor performance in packing does not imply poor potential in rebin, teaching, robotics or other roles. Provide supported reassignment and skills trials rather than default punishment.
- Employees can share or offer their own expertise through **ZeroMarket**, including pottery, technical skills and creative work, under voluntary terms.

## Intellectual property and entrepreneurship
- **The employee/inventor owns inventions and creative output**, including work created during exploration time, with employer resources, or with exceptional commercial value, as ZeroCloud's contractual policy. No automatic assignment to the company.
- Any ZeroCloud use, distribution, incorporation or commercialization requires a **separate, informed, voluntary negotiated license or purchase with compensation**. Refusal may not trigger retaliation, loss of hours or benefits.
- ZeroVentures may transparently fund employee businesses or ideas without requiring compulsory transfer of ownership. Record funding, consent, IP scope, revenue share if any, termination rights and conflict-of-interest terms.
- Actual employment/IP agreements and statutory rules require specialist legal review before real-world deployment; the design intent is not a claim that default law already guarantees these outcomes.

## Earned Experience Credits
- Experience Credits (ZeroPay ledger) are **wage-like earned compensation**, accruing at exactly **0.5 credits per eligible paid hour**.
- All eligible paid time accrues regardless of title, performance, discipline, manager opinion, reassignment or assigned department. No discretionary forfeiture, arbitrary clawback or selective denial.
- Every employee can inspect their ledger, source paid hours, accrual formula, transfers, redemptions and disputes. Support correction, audit and appeal.
- Transfers to a partner or chosen family are supported under explicit employee authorization.
- Paid-hours definition and redemption/tax/legal treatment require documented jurisdictional review. Do not misrepresent simulated credits as real money or a real benefit.

## Resort access and amenities
- Employee resort amenities are contractual benefits with a published **equal baseline**, transparent capacity allocation, eligibility and reservation rules, protected personal use, appeal rights and reserved inventory.
- Employee guest mode and authorized partner/chosen-family participation are first-class workflows. Benefits cannot be withdrawn merely because a manager dislikes an employee.
- Where capacity is limited, use transparent published rules rather than hidden discretionary favoritism.

## Acceptance tests
1. Paid exploration balance cannot be debited by mandatory training.
2. Employee with weak performance in one role can apply for paid learning or transfer to another.
3. Invention made using company equipment remains inventor-owned in the ZeroCloud contractual model; corporate use requires a separately recorded license.
4. 40 eligible paid hours accrue exactly 20 Experience Credits; 36 hours accrue exactly 18; manager performance flags cannot change the formula.
5. Credit transfer to chosen family is authorized, auditable and reversible only under published correction/dispute rules.
6. Amenities show equal baseline, published capacity rules and appeal path; protected personal use is not surveilled.
7. Simulator produces synthetic event receipts and replayable accounting; no claim of production payroll integration.
